1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the K.G.N. Fashion Zone Android catalogue app and its associated website, operated for the K.G.N. Fashion Zone wholesale garments business in Khalilabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. It covers the customer catalogue, retailer registration and login, enquiries, direct checkout, order tracking, saved details and contacting the store. Store administration is a separate restricted service; the customer app does not include an admin login entry.

Customers can browse the public catalogue without an account. Wholesale prices and new orders require an approved retailer account. Direct online checkout checks stock and prices automatically and opens Razorpay’s hosted payment page without waiting for a separate store confirmation. The current direct checkout collects the item amount online; delivery charges are paid separately to the courier and communicated separately. Existing orders retain their agreed shipping terms. The Order privacy notice explains the additional information, delivery providers, payment processing and retention that apply to these requests. This policy also explains when an action opens an independent service such as WhatsApp or Google Maps.

2. Information handled by the app

Catalogue, language and selections

The app stores a language preference and enquiry selections, including product identifiers, sizes, packs and quantities, in browser storage on your device. Public page files and images may be cached to improve loading. Product searches are performed in the page; the current app does not send the typed search text to the catalogue database.

Information you choose to include in an enquiry

You may optionally enter your name or shop name and city or market. These fields, along with selected products, sizes and quantities, are used to prepare an enquiry. The current catalogue client does not upload these form fields to its product database.

When you tap the WhatsApp enquiry button, the generated message is passed to WhatsApp through a link to prepare a draft. WhatsApp processes the link when opened. The store receives the message when you send it and may receive the phone number, profile name and other information visible through your WhatsApp settings. Messages or other details you independently send to the store are also received by the store.

Sharing, calls and external links

The general Share app feature supplies the public app link and an introductory message to your chosen sharing service. It does not include your enquiry selections. Copy actions write the requested link or enquiry text to your device clipboard; the app does not read your clipboard history. Telephone and map links open the relevant external service. Developer-contact links open a separate app-development enquiry to CityReach Solutions; they do not include your garment enquiry. If you choose to contact that developer, the information you send is handled by that recipient. External services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Technical request information

Loading pages, product images and software makes network requests to the providers listed below. These providers receive information needed to serve a request, such as an IP address and request details, and may retain operational or security logs. This is separate from the product enquiry you choose to send.

Retailer registration, login and orders

Retailer registration stores your mobile number, shop and contact name, address, city, optional GST number, consent and shop photo or visiting card privately in Supabase. Authorised staff review applications. Approval grants wholesale access; it does not prove government verification or mobile OTP verification. Supabase Auth handles your password and account identity. Retailer login sessions persist in browser storage until logout or expiry. Staff cannot view your existing password; after checking identity they can issue a temporary password or a one-time recovery code.

Orders store the buyer/contact name, phone, delivery address, selected products and sizes, quantities, prices, status events and payment references. Private tracking keys remain on your device; the database stores their hashes. Optional mobile verification shares the number with Supabase Auth and the configured SMS provider only when you choose to request an OTP. It is not required to order. The app does not store card credentials, CVVs, banking passwords or UPI PINs.

Separate store administration

Restricted administration pages use Supabase authentication for an authorised owner. An administrator submits an email address and password for authentication. A session token is stored in the browser's session storage. Selected product photos, product information, stock changes and notes are stored in Supabase; published catalogue content and photos are available to customers. Retailer accounts described above are separate from these staff accounts.

3. Purposes and service providers

The app handles the information described above to display the catalogue, remember selections, prepare enquiries, review retailer applications, authenticate customers, process payments and orders, arrange delivery, provide support and operate the service reliably.

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and public app files. GitHub documents that it records visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Supabase: provides the catalogue, retailer and order databases, private shop-proof storage, product-image storage, authentication and related operational logs. Supabase privacy notice and data processing terms.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the Supabase software library. Its network may process request information such as IP address, browser details and access time. jsDelivr privacy policy.
  • Razorpay: processes payments on its hosted checkout, receives payment/order details and confirms results to the store. Its privacy policy applies. Necessary delivery contact details are shared with the selected courier.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: when available, requires separate Google account authorisation for the app’s private backup folder. It is not enabled merely by registering. Details and customer controls are in the order privacy notice.
  • Services you open: WhatsApp, Gmail or another email app, messaging services, Google Maps and the service chosen through the device share menu process the information passed to them and information you provide there. Their own privacy notices govern that processing.

These providers may process information in countries outside your own. The current app has no integrated advertising SDK or dedicated behavioural analytics SDK. Provider operational logs still exist.

K.G.N. Fashion Zone keeps customer enquiries in WhatsApp. The store does not maintain separate registers or Excel copies of WhatsApp enquiries and does not sell or rent customer information. In-app orders, when enabled, are stored in a private Supabase order database. Necessary delivery information is shared with the selected courier. The order privacy notice describes this processing separately from WhatsApp enquiries. This does not remove the technical processing by the providers described above or the information you choose to send through an external service.

4. Security and device access

The app's website, catalogue and authentication endpoints use HTTPS. Owner administration requires authentication and an authorised account. Access credentials must be protected by the account holder. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

The current Android build does not request sensitive permissions to read contacts, the SMS inbox, microphone, camera or precise device location. Retailers can deliberately select a shop photo or visiting card through the system file picker. Supported browsers may offer optional OTP autofill with user consent; the Android wrapper does not request SMS inbox access. Opening a third-party app may involve permissions controlled by that third party and your device settings. The app does not automatically place calls, send messages or read your conversations.

5. Retention and deletion

  • On-device selections and settings: remain until changed or cleared by the user, removed by browser/device storage management, or replaced by an app update. Clearing the website's data removes local settings, selections and caches. Uninstalling the Android wrapper may not clear the website data held separately by a browser.
  • Retailer account: contact details, application and login identity remain while the account is used unless deletion is requested. Staff can remove the proof photo after approval without deleting the account.
  • Orders: when automatic cleanup is enabled, operational records are deleted in the next daily cleanup one calendar year after delivery or cancellation. Refund/dispute holds prevent routine deletion. Limited payment and inventory records remain separately as described in the order privacy notice.
  • Saved details and backups: opt-in delivery details and up to 100 receipt copies remain in this browser until removed. Downloaded files and copies in your own Google Drive must be managed separately. Background backup while the app is closed is not guaranteed.
  • Sessions: sign out to end use on a shared device. Clearing local data alone does not delete the server-side account.
  • Provider logs: are subject to the relevant provider's service settings, retention practices and applicable requirements. The app does not implement its own deletion schedule for provider infrastructure logs.

Customer WhatsApp enquiries: one year

K.G.N. Fashion Zone keeps customer WhatsApp enquiry messages for one year from the date received. At the end of that period, the store deletes the enquiry information it retains in WhatsApp. The store does not keep a separate customer register or Excel copy.

Delete your account and associated data

Request account and data deletion. This public page works without login or app installation. It is also linked from the customer account screen and the app’s More section. Contact the store with your registered mobile number and shop name; an order ID is optional. We verify identity and normally complete verified requests within 30 days, confirming the result.

Deletion covers the retailer authentication account, profile, application, remaining shop proof and associated personal data no longer needed. Ongoing order/delivery/refund/dispute information and limited records required for accounting, legal or fraud-prevention reasons may be retained as necessary; we explain the reason and retention period. Other eligible data need not wait for routine one-year cleanup. Blocking an account or deleting only its proof photo does not count as account deletion.

Request earlier deletion of WhatsApp enquiries

You may request deletion before that year ends by calling or sending a WhatsApp message to +91 7905275441. Say that you want to delete your K.G.N. Fashion Zone enquiry information. To help locate the messages, provide the WhatsApp number you used and, if known, the approximate enquiry date. Do not send passwords, OTPs or identity documents.

K.G.N. Fashion Zone handles the request and may confirm it through the number associated with the enquiry to avoid deleting another person's data. After that check, the store deletes the enquiry data under its control. If applicable law requires retaining particular information, the store will explain the reason and retain only what is required.

Clearing local app data does not remove messages or copies already held by the store or another service. Requests concerning your independent WhatsApp, Google or other service account should also be made through that service. Deleting the retailer account does not automatically erase files downloaded to your phone or backups in your own Google Drive.

The catalogue is intended for wholesale buyers and business users. Displaying children's garments does not make the service a children's app. If information about a child has been sent to the store, contact the privacy contact below so it can be addressed using the contact and deletion process in this policy.
